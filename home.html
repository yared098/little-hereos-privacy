Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Introduction

Welcome to Little Heroes ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Little Heroes mobile application and web administration platform.

Little Heroes is intended for adults only. Our users are school administrators, teachers, and parents who are 18 years of age or older. Children do not create accounts or use the application directly.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information from adult users:

  • Phone Number: Used for OTP (one-time password) verification during login
  • Account Information: Name, email address, and role (admin, teacher, or parent) as registered by the school administrator
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and feature usage within the app
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device type

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Verify your identity through OTP authentication
  • Provide and improve our educational services
  • Enable teachers and parents to access classroom and student information
  • Communicate important updates and notifications
  • Ensure platform safety and prevent misuse
  • Comply with legal requirements

Student Information

Student information (such as name, class, and learning progress) is entered into our platform by authorized teachers and school administrators. This information is:

  • Provided only by the school or authorized teachers
  • Accessible only to the school, its teachers, and the student's parent or guardian
  • Never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes

Parents may request access, correction, or deletion of their child's information by contacting their school administrator or emailing us at info@littleheroesethiopia.com.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, SMS delivery for OTP)
  • The school that owns your account
  • Legal authorities when required by law

Data Security

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information. However, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

As an adult user, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions or concerns

Children's Privacy

Little Heroes is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts or use the application. Any student information in the platform is entered solely by authorized teachers and school administrators with the consent of parents or guardians.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us at info@littleheroesethiopia.com and we will promptly delete it.

Cookies

We may use cookies to improve your experience on our web platform. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
